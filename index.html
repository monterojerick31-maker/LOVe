6 MONTHS OF US ♡

For the person I love ♡

Six months down. Hopefully, many more to go.

Thank you for staying with me for these six months.

Hindi ko man laging nasasabi nang maayos, pero sobrang thankful ako na dumating ka sa buhay ko. Thank you sa pag-intindi, sa pag-stay, sa mga tawanan, sa random moments, at kahit sa mga araw na hindi tayo okay.

Alam kong hindi ako perfect. Minsan toxic ako, mabilis magtampo, makulit, overthinker, at kapag nagmamahal ako, grabe ako magmahal. May mga pagkakataon na baka nakakapagod ako. May times na hindi ko alam kung paano sabihin nang tama yung nararamdaman ko.

Pero I want you to know that I'm trying. I'm trying to understand you better. I'm trying to control the things that can hurt you. And I'm trying to become the kind of person who can love you without making love feel heavy.

Thank you for staying even when I'm not the easiest person to handle. Thank you for choosing to understand me. Thank you for giving me reasons to smile, and thank you for becoming one of the people I never want to lose.

Six months may sound short to other people, but for me, ang dami na nating memories. And I don't want the story to end here.

Sana magtuloy-tuloy pa tayo. Sana marami pa tayong months, birthdays, random conversations, tampuhan na naaayos, tawanan, late-night talks, at ordinary days na nagiging special simply because we're together.

If ever I become too much, please remember this: behind all my kulit, tampo, and pagiging toxic sometimes is someone who genuinely loves you and is still learning how to love you better.

So thank you. Thank you for staying. Thank you for knowing me. Thank you for loving me.

Happy 6 months to us. ♡
Here's to more memories, more chances, more growth, and hopefully a much longer story together.

I love you.
And I'll keep trying to love you better.
